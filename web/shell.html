CHIP-8 · emulator and debugger

Written from scratch in C++ with a Raylib front end, compiled to WebAssembly. The panel on the right is live machine state: registers, stack, the bytes around I, and a disassembly that follows the program counter.

It opens on Brix, a brick breaker — move with A and D. Tab cycles to Pong (1/Q for the left player, 4/R for the right) and Catch (A and D again), then on to three smaller test ROMs. Every one of them is original, written in CHIP-8 assembly and kept as readable source in roms/src/.

Click the canvas first so it takes the keyboard. The 16-key pad maps to 1 2 3 4 Q W E R A S D F Z X C V. Space pauses, N single steps while paused, Tab cycles the bundled ROMs, Backspace resets, [ and ] change speed, and F1–F5 toggle the hardware quirks.
